# Meow or Never

Made entirely in 4 days for the GMTK game jam 2026 by me (Theme: Countdown)

How to play: download the zip file and extract it. Go to the newly created folder, and run the .exe file inside!
### Link: [https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-jam-2026/rate/4822820](url)

## Story:

Two cats named Cloud and Bean are close friends that live in log houses deep in the forest. One day when Bean arrives to their usual hangout spot, Cloud is nowhere to be found. After searching, Bean finds a mysterious sticky note from Cloud:

"I'm sorry I couldn't say goodbye. I'm leaving today at 8am"

Bean realizes that Cloud is moving away with his human and plans to deliver a letter to say goodbye. He writes a letter, makes his own car out of cardboard and sets out to give Cloud his letter and say goodbye!

As the time counts down towards Cloud's flight, Bean has to hurry to make it in time while being careful with his fragile cardboard car.

### Controls:

WASD to move (or triggers and joystick for controller)

Hold space to drift (or X for PS and A for Xbox controllers)

### Credits:

I made the cat, car, tire, navigation arrow and location icon 3D models in Blender. I used materials from BlenderKit for these models.

I used LowPoly WinterPack Lite (Finotti Games) for the log houses

Low Poly Environment (Polytope Studio) for the terrain including trees, rocks and grass

CliffStonePackM (Dzeruza) for the rocky cliffs near the center of the map

Music Credit: http://www.crazyduckgames.de (crazy duck games)
